# Customer Call Response Guide (for new starters)

Built from real call recordings. For each type of question you'll get on the phone, this shows the **standard way to handle it** and **real examples of how the team handled it**. Use it to learn the common questions and expected responses.

After-sales note: for damaged / faulty / wrong / missing items and returns, ask the customer to email photos + their order number to **support@example.com**. Warranty and change-of-mind returns: point them to the website (User Centre / Contact Us).

## Product Info & Availability  (318 calls)

**Standard responses:**

- **Product Specifications & Features** — Provide detailed product specifications, features, materials, and any relevant technical information. If the information is not readily available, transfer to a product specialist.  _(escalate to: Sales)_
- **Product Availability & Stock** — Check current stock levels for the requested product, including specific colors or variants. Provide lead times for out-of-stock items or special orders. Offer alternative products if the requested item is unavailable.  _(escalate to: Warehouse / stock)_
- **Customization & Modification** — Inform the customer about available customization options, lead times, and potential costs. If customisation is not offered, clearly state this. For complex requests, transfer to a specialist.  _(escalate to: Sales)_
- **Assembly & Installation** — Provide assembly instructions, information on installation services (if available), and associated costs or regional restrictions. For specific order-related instructions, locate the order first.  _(escalate to: After-sales / support)_
- **Parts & Identification** — Assist with identifying products or specific parts. For replacement parts, check availability and process for ordering. If identification is difficult, request SKU or images.  _(escalate to: After-sales / support)_
- **Pricing & Delivery Costs** — Provide product pricing and generate delivery cost quotes based on the customer's postcode. Inform about any regional delivery restrictions or depot locations.  _(escalate to: Delivery / freight)_

**Real examples from calls:**

- *"The customer is inquiring about the price, stock availability, and pickup/delivery options for a greenhouse."*
  -> The agent confirmed the price and stock availability of the product and provided pickup and delivery options.
- *"The customer called to confirm if the door on a steel cabinet can be re-hinged to open from the left side."*
  -> The agent confirmed that the door on the steel cabinet can be hinged on either the left or right side.
- *"The customer is asking for the measurement of the overhang/apron on the SKU-9841 Hampton's French provincial table."*
  -> The agent provided an approximate measurement and explained the feature, but could not give an exact dimension.
- *"The customer is inquiring about the possibility of removing the feet from a CO12P cabinet to reduce its height due to ceiling limitations in their shed."*
  -> The agent will check the product specifications regarding the feet and their impact on the cabinet height and functionality, then call the customer back.
- *"The customer is inquiring about the availability and price of three bookcases (SKU-1162) after confirming an earlier purchase."*
  -> The agent put the caller on hold to check the stock level for the requested item.
- *"The customer is inquiring about a specific black display cabinet with glass doors, its availability, and delivery to North Queensland."*
  -> The agent helped the caller identify the product by asking for details like color, material, size, and price, then sent a picture to the caller's mobile for confirmation.

## Quotes & Pricing  (253 calls)

**Standard responses:**

- **Product & Delivery Quotes** — To provide an accurate quote for products and delivery, we need the specific product details (SKU, size, color), the quantity desired, and the full delivery postcode. This information allows us to calculate both product pricing and associated shipping costs. For complex or custom requests, a sales representative will need to generate a detailed quote.  _(escalate to: Sales)_
- **Quote Management & Clarification** — If you have an existing quote, please provide the quote number or the email address used to receive it. We can then clarify details, resend the quote, or assist with modifications. For follow-ups, we can check the status of your quote.  _(escalate to: Sales)_
- **Installation & Assembly Quotes** — For quotes on installation or assembly services, we need details about the product, your location, and any specific site conditions. This often requires a review by our warehouse or installation team. We will collect your details and arrange for a specialist to contact you with a quote.  _(escalate to: Warehouse / stock)_
- **Pricing Negotiation & Discounts** — For price negotiation, bulk discounts, or price match requests, these inquiries are handled by our sales management team. Please provide details of your request, and we will connect you with the appropriate person.  _(escalate to: Sales)_
- **Pricing Discrepancies & Policy** — If you've noticed a price discrepancy or have questions about our pricing policies (e.g., sale end dates, holding stock), please provide the details. We will investigate the discrepancy or clarify our policies. For complex issues, a manager may need to review.  _(escalate to: a Manager)_
- **Trade & Wholesale Pricing** — For trade accounts or wholesale pricing inquiries, please provide your business details. We will connect you with our sales team who can assist with applications and provide specific wholesale pricing information.  _(escalate to: Sales)_

- *"The customer is calling to get a price on a shed (GD3691B) and is inquiring about shipping options to Brisbane, specifically to a depot."*

## Order Placement  (162 calls)

**Standard responses:**

- **Order Initiation & Placement** — Assist the customer in placing a new order, including confirming product details, stock availability, and initial pricing. If a specific sales representative is already assisting the customer, transfer the call to them.  _(escalate to: Sales)_
- **Order Modification & Additions** — Retrieve the existing order details and process requests to add items, modify quantities, or change product specifications. Confirm any impact on pricing or delivery and process additional payments if necessary.  _(escalate to: Sales)_
- **Payment & Invoicing** — Process deposit or full payments, provide payment links, confirm payment methods, and send invoices. If complex payment arrangements are required (e.g., split payments, Afterpay), transfer to accounts.  _(escalate to: Accounts / invoicing)_
- **Stock & Product Information** — Provide real-time stock availability, product dimensions, specifications, and confirm assembly instructions. If detailed product information or custom configurations are needed, transfer to sales.  _(escalate to: Warehouse / stock)_
- **Delivery & Freight** — Calculate delivery costs, provide delivery options, and confirm delivery company names. For complex freight quotes or specific delivery arrangements, transfer to the freight team.  _(escalate to: Delivery / freight)_
- **Order Confirmation & Status** — Confirm order details, send order confirmations, and provide updates on order status. If a customer is awaiting a specific confirmation or has a unique request, escalate to a manager.  _(escalate to: Sales)_
- **Agent & Contact Information** — Provide agent contact details or direct the customer to the appropriate contact person or department for their specific query.

## Payments & Refunds  (100 calls)

**Standard responses:**

- **Make a Payment** — Provide available payment options (bank transfer, credit card, payment link, cash on pickup). Explain any surcharges. Offer to send an invoice or payment link. If taking payment over the phone, ensure compliance with security protocols and amount limits.
- **Payment Issues & Declines** — For declined payments, advise the customer to try another method or contact their bank. If a payment link was not received, resend it. For credit card issues, suggest trying again or using an alternative payment method. If a refund has already been issued, consult with accounts.  _(escalate to: Accounts / invoicing)_
- **Payment Confirmation & Receipts** — Confirm payment status by checking the system. If payment is confirmed, advise on next steps (e.g., dispatch). For bank transfers, request remittance advice. Resend receipts to the customer's preferred email or phone number.  _(escalate to: Accounts / invoicing)_
- **Refund Status & Processing** — Check the status of the refund in the system. Advise the customer on the expected timeframe for the refund to appear in their account (e.g., 3-5 business days). If further investigation is needed, transfer to accounts or offer to follow up.  _(escalate to: Accounts / invoicing)_
- **Invoice & Payment Details** — Provide invoice details, including outstanding balance and payment options. Resend invoices as requested. For discrepancies or special requests (e.g., payment plans, due date extensions, discounts), note the request and escalate to the appropriate department.  _(escalate to: Accounts / invoicing)_

**Real examples from calls:**

Some exclusions apply, such as custom orders or items marked as final sale. Refunds are typically processed within a few business days to the original payment method. Exchanges are subject to stock availability and may incur a restocking fee. Please refer to our full policy on our website for detailed information.
- **Order Cancellation** — To cancel an order, please email our support team at support@example.com with your order number and reason for cancellation. Please note that a non-refundable deposit may apply, and cancellation fees may be incurred depending on the order status and product type. We will review your request and advise on the next steps.  _(escalate to: After-sales / support)_
- **Damaged/Faulty Item** — If you have received a damaged or faulty item, please email photos of the damage/fault along with your order number to our support team at support@example.com within the applicable period of delivery. We will assess the issue and arrange for a replacement, repair, or refund as appropriate. Please retain all original packaging.  _(escalate to: After-sales / support)_
- **Exchange Request** — To request an exchange, please contact our support team at support@example.com with your order number and details of the item you wish to exchange, along with the item you would like instead. Exchanges are subject to stock availability and may incur a restocking fee. We will advise on the process and any applicable fees.  _(escalate to: After-sales / support)_
- **Return/Repair Status** — To check the status of your return or repair, please provide your return/repair reference number or original order number. We will then look up the current status and estimated timeline for resolution. Please allow a few business days for updates after an item has been received by our warehouse.  _(escalate to: Warehouse / stock)_
- **Fee Dispute** — If you wish to dispute a cancellation fee, restocking fee, or any other charge, please email a detailed explanation of your dispute along with your order number to our sales team at the sales team. A sales representative will review your case and contact you to discuss further.  _(escalate to: Sales)_

## Callback & Specific Contact  (335 calls)

**Standard responses:**

- **General Callback Request** — Confirm the caller's name and phone number. Ask for a brief reason for the callback to ensure the correct person or department can assist. Arrange for a suitable colleague to return the call.  _(escalate to: Sales)_
- **Specific Person Contact/Callback** — Attempt to transfer the call directly to the requested person. If unavailable, confirm their availability and offer to arrange a callback or take a message. If contact details are requested, provide only publicly available information (e.g., general email address for a department) or offer to forward the request.  _(escalate to: the specific person / callback)_
- **Missed Call Follow-up** — Identify the colleague who made the missed call and the reason for it. Attempt to connect the caller to that colleague or arrange a callback. If the issue is resolved, confirm with the caller.  _(escalate to: the specific person / callback)_
- **Product/Service Information Callback** — Gather details about the product or service in question. Offer to arrange a callback from a specialist (e.g., sales, technical support) who can provide the requested information or assistance.  _(escalate to: Sales)_
- **Financial/Logistical Callback** — Gather necessary details (e.g., order number, invoice number, delivery address). Offer to arrange a callback from the relevant department (e.g., accounts, delivery, warehouse) after they have investigated the query.  _(escalate to: Delivery / freight)_
- **Email/Communication Follow-up** — Confirm the caller's email address and the subject of the email. Offer to resend the email or check its status. If a specific action is required (e.g., signing an agreement), confirm the next steps.  _(escalate to: After-sales / support)_
- **Manager Request** — Ascertain the reason for wanting to speak with a manager. If appropriate, attempt to transfer the call. Otherwise, offer to take a message and arrange a callback from a manager.  _(escalate to: a Manager)_
